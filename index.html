About Amariah

Training built around you.

Fitness is personal — and so is the way I coach.

I’m Amariah, a personal trainer focused on helping people build real strength, move better, and feel more confident in their bodies. My approach blends structured strength work with mobility, conditioning and consistent, honest coaching.

Every program starts with where you actually are — your body, your goals, and your schedule — not a one-size-fits-all plan. Whether you’re just starting out or looking to push further, training is built around what you need next.

I believe good training is sustainable training: progressive, adaptable, and grounded in movement quality — not just effort for the sake of it.

Train with purpose. Move with confidence.
